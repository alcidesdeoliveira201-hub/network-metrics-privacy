Network Metrics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Network Metrics is a private desktop tool used exclusively by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels.

  1. Data accessed. With the owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the tool accesses the owner's own YouTube channel data: video metadata, upload and scheduling settings, captions, comments posted by the owner, and channel analytics (YouTube Analytics and Reporting APIs).
  2. Use. The data is used only to upload and manage the owner's videos and to produce internal performance reports.
  3. Storage. Data and OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No servers, no databases, no third parties.
  4. Sharing. No data is shared, sold or transferred to anyone.
  5. Other users. The tool has no other users and collects no personal data from viewers or other channels. Public statistics of other channels (view counts, upload dates) may be read in aggregate for editorial research and are not stored beyond 30 days.
  6. Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.
  7. Contact. Through the YouTube channels' contact information.

This application uses YouTube API Services.
